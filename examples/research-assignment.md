# Example: a bounded research assignment

**Synthetic illustration.** Every source card, exchange, artifact and result below is invented to demonstrate the method. This is not a report of installed or tested paired operation. It does not replace the first runtime path through Chief of Staff and Head of Talent.

The [Research Lead](../roles/research-lead.md) works under the [common operating contract](../docs/operating-contract.md). One role has two participants, one accepted current basis and one outward continuation. The example shows how their differing interpretations affect the work.

## Assignment and shared starting basis

Chief of Staff commissions a recommendation on which research-record handover method to test. The intended outcome is to resume interrupted work without losing accepted human steering or making the human reconstruct it.

| Assignment field | Accepted basis, revision 1 |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting and decision owner | Research Lead reports to Chief of Staff. Head of Harness Engineering owns any later implementation test. |
| Scope | Compare fictional Candidates A and B from the four supplied source cards. Recommend a bounded test or an explicit no-action disposition. |
| Exclusions | No installation, account changes, external messages or inference about private community discussions. |
| Permitted actions | Read the supplied evidence and public reference routes. Write the recommendation to the assigned isolated work record. |
| Effort and checkpoint | One 45-minute research block. Exchange findings after first evidence review and before delivery. |
| Deliverable and acceptance | A recommendation, evidence and limits, retained alternatives, and an implementation question. It must preserve accepted steering and distinguish documented capability from tested behaviour. |
| Stop and return | Stop at the effort limit. Return unresolved acceptance issues or proposed scope changes through Chief of Staff. |

Both agents receive the same contract, brief revision, source cards, available capabilities and record routes. They read the relevant guidance. Agent A initially adopts the doer/giver relational role and owns the shared write. Agent B adopts the observer/active-receiver relational role. These complementary polarities may reverse while the enduring Research Lead role and contract remain the same. Both may investigate and propose changes. Reversing polarity does not itself transfer the shared write or confer new authority.

The question triggers [Community Research](../skills/community-research/SKILL.md), which routes to relevant practitioner and implementation evidence. [LISTS](../LISTS.md) supplies resource navigation, not a replacement brief. [Sense of Taste](../skills/sense-of-taste/SKILL.md) supplies judgement during the work. Writing and Editing applies to the resulting recommendation. The pair retrieves the relevant material rather than loading every collection.

## Evidence and differing interpretations

These source cards stand in for originals in this illustration. A real assignment would retain their exact URLs or authorised source locations and the passages actually read.

| Card | Fictional evidence | Reliance limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Candidate A's guide includes accepted brief revision, completed items and unresolved effects in its handover record. | Documented fields only; no runtime test. |
| E2 | Candidate B's demo uses fewer handover steps, but its current record omits accepted steering. | A selected demonstration does not establish lower total human effort. |
| E3 | A maintainer reply proposes adding steering to Candidate B's next release. | A proposal is not a shipped capability. |
| E4 | The supplied Candidate A sample includes brief revision 1 and separate provisional notes from each participant. | One synthetic sample does not establish reliable recovery. |

Agent A initially favours B because its demo is shorter. Agent B reads E1–E4 before A's verdict and identifies the mismatch: the assignment concerns preserving steering, and E2 does not establish that B can do so. A accepts the finding, revises the recommendation towards A as the initial test candidate, and retains B's simpler interaction as a possible benefit to investigate.

Agent B also claims that A lacks a way to identify the current brief. A points to the exact revision field in E1 and E4. B withdraws that objection. The supported criticism changes A's conclusion; the mistaken criticism does not erase A's useful evidence. Neither finding is accepted because of which agent made it.

E3 remains a useful possibility. B proposes checking the next release only if the work later warrants it. The pair records that possibility without treating it as current functionality or creating a monitoring schedule.

## Human steering changes the whole comparison

The human clarifies: “Preserving the brief matters, but the result I care about is less time reconstructing my intention. Include my effort in the test.” Chief of Staff records this as brief revision 2 and supplies it to both participants.

Both acknowledge revision 2 and apply it. Their recommendation now asks Engineering to test recovery accuracy and the human's clarification and repair effort. Counting saved fields alone would miss the clarified outcome. They keep E1's useful capability evidence, the warning about B's omitted steering and B's possible interaction advantage. They amend the comparison, proposed test and acceptance conditions together, preserving the prior revision.

The existing work record distinguishes the accepted basis from provisional views. It records A's initial preference, B's supported challenge, B's withdrawn objection and the unresolved possibility in E3. It does not retain private reasoning traces or promote either agent's preference into an instruction.

## Interruption, uncertain write and resumption

A writes recommendation `R-001` revision 2 to the assigned isolated record, then receives a timeout before acknowledgement. A does not assume failure or repeat the write. The saved checkpoint contains:

| Checkpoint field | Exact state |
| --- | --- |
| Completed | E1–E4 read; both findings resolved; brief revision 2 received and applied by both agents; recommendation prepared. |
| Uncertain effect | Write of `R-001` revision 2 to the assigned destination may have succeeded. Agent A owns reconciliation. |
| Pending | Inspect that exact record; confirm artifact content and revision; deliver the handover to Chief of Staff and establish receipt. |
| Preserved perspectives | B's withdrawn objection and E3's unresolved alternative remain distinguishable from the accepted recommendation. |
| Next authorised move | Read `R-001` at the same destination before considering a retry. No installation or external action is authorised. |

B becomes unavailable before handover. The role records degraded operation and preserves the checkpoint. Chief of Staff assigns a replacement, who receives the accepted basis, original evidence, material interpretations and exact remainder. The replacement confirms receipt and explains how revision 2 changes the acceptance question. Merely sending the packet would not establish restoration.

A reads `R-001` at the exact destination. Its saved content matches recommendation revision 2, so no repeat write is needed. The restored receiver checks the saved artifact against the brief. A then delivers one coherent handover. Chief of Staff acknowledges receipt. Artifact saving and handover delivery are separate completed items.

## Delivery and later feedback

The recommendation is accepted as a research deliverable: test A first, retain B as an alternative, and measure recovery accuracy together with human repair effort. No runtime superiority has been established. Engineering still needs a separately assigned test using the same sources, tools and standards for a well-briefed single agent and the pair.

Chief of Staff owns the pending response: whether to commission that test. If commissioned, Engineering returns the findings within the agreed test window, including adverse results and the whole effort. Research Lead revisits the recommendation when those findings arrive, when relevant capability evidence changes, or at the next agreed review if no response has arrived. Silence remains unknown; it does not prove rejection or success. This example creates no calendar event or recurring job.

The retained lesson is specific: a shorter demonstration did not answer the commissioned continuity question, and the human's clarification changed how success should be assessed. Research Lead records the lesson with this evidence and the next relevant retrieval trigger. Its value remains to be checked on a later assignment where the recipient actually retrieves it, applies it correctly and observes the result.
