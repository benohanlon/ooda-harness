## Meaning and sources

Odysseus supplies the engineer's chosen archetype. The Phaeacian ship is the metaphor for a harness carrying the sovereign's intent toward their destination. The distant island represents Ithaca. A small thread of hearth smoke expresses homecoming. Wilson's Book 1 names smoke from his homeland rather than his own hearth specifically. [Literary and visual sources](../../lists/odysseus-visual-resources.md).

## Production and continuity

The original 5 November 2026 illustration was generated with the built-in image tool from the sovereign's supplied woodcut reference, then edited to add the dwelling and smoke. Its warm-paper, oxblood and vermilion version remains in Git history. This is a poetic illustration, not an archaeological reconstruction or reproduction of an identified historical print.

The new image-tool edit preserves the square sail, crew, stormy sea, original cut-line texture, distant Ithaca and smoke. Petrol-teal shadows and mint highlights replace warm paper; restrained orange-red preserves the ship's red cheek. Live type supplies the role and the proposed line 'Find a way home.' That is role copy, not a quotation from Homer or Wilson. The card uses the Engineering role label; no further pack number is assigned.

The [brand guidelines](../../brand/GUIDELINES.md) own the common visual system. Artwork changes do not alter the role's contract, source binding or installation.
