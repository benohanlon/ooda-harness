# Odysseus cover

Approved original cover for [Head of Harness Engineering](../../roles/head-of-harness-engineering.md), selected 5 November 2026. [PNG](head-of-harness-engineering-odysseus.png).

## Meaning and sources

Odysseus supplies the engineer's chosen archetype. The Phaeacian ship is our metaphor for a harness carrying the sovereign's intent towards their destination. The distant island represents Ithaca. A small thread of hearth-smoke expresses homecoming. Wilson's Book 1 names smoke from his homeland rather than his own hearth specifically. [Literary and visual sources](../../lists/odysseus-visual-resources.md).
