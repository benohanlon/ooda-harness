# Roles

Roles carry continuing responsibility for outcomes. This directory summarises the ten agreed role types in OODA Harness. The [common operating contract](docs/operating-contract.md), [role template](roles/ROLE-TEMPLATE.md) and [Research Lead contract](roles/research-lead.md) are prepared for review and manual testing. Other individual contracts and all paired-role runtime loading and execution remain unverified or unfinished.

The [README](README.md) explains the wider operating model. [SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md) maps shared methods and their implementation status.

## First implementation

Start with a bounded Chief of Staff assignment using Perception Check, then a career assignment through Chief of Staff and Head of Talent. Head of Harness Engineering owns the build and its reliability. Add further roles when continuing work needs a distinct owner. The ten role types below do not imply ten deployed roles or twenty always-on agents.

## Agreed responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Chief of Staff** | Maintain the whole picture: orientation, priorities, coordination, challenge and review. Integrate specialist findings and bring you progress, recommendations and decisions that need your judgement. |
| **Head of Talent** | Own the career remit, including opportunities, recruiters, assessment, applications, interviews and progression. This combines the earlier Career Lead, Executive Recruiter and Executive Career Agent responsibilities. |
| **Head of Business Development** | Win worthwhile fractional and client work, from understanding buyer problems and selecting an offer through qualification, proposals, terms and commercial follow-through. |
| **Creative Director** | Own creative direction and quality across writing, graphics, memes, animation and video. Preserve argument, research fidelity, voice and meaning, while keeping each publication or project's brief, brand, evidence and assets distinct. |
| **Project Lead — per project** | Own a project's agreed outcomes, scope, workplan, dependencies, stakeholders, delivery and results. Use the same role definition with a separate private brief and state for each project. |
| **Caseworker** | Manage continuing cases involving rules, laws, standards or complex bureaucratic processes. Maintain each case's evidence, correspondence, deadlines, decisions and next action. |
| **Head of Harness Engineering** | Build and maintain reusable definitions, integrations, collectors, runtime mapping, checks and reliability. Conduct bounded independent reviews of whole-system alignment, drift and design effectiveness. |
| **Personal Assistant** | Handle general administration, appointments, practical requests and routing. Continuing evidence-led cases belong to Caseworker; wider judgement and coordination belong to Chief of Staff. |
| **Head of Finance** | Own financial planning, including portfolio and risk analysis. Its contract will define the detailed remit and authority. Keep records and risk calculations separate for personal holdings and funded challenges. |
| **[Research Lead](roles/research-lead.md)** | Own commissioned inquiry, exploratory discovery and the organisation of useful research. Connect evidence to the decisions or work it informs, retain useful knowledge and seek later feedback. Other roles retain their domain decisions and can conduct research. |

Head of Business Development wins client mandates; Project Leads deliver the agreed work. Where career and commercial opportunities overlap, assign one current owner and preserve the existing history.

## Coordination and shared skills

Each enduring role is realised by two agents around one contract and outward accountability. Both start from the same relevant specification, guidance, context and available capabilities, then adopt complementary relational roles, or polarities: doer/giver and observer/active receiver. These positions may reverse within the same enduring role and contract. Each has a complete OODA and can change the other's understanding during work. The [common operating contract](docs/operating-contract.md) owns their decision rights, one action owner per work unit, checkpoints and recovery. Research Lead has its own pair and is not the other roles' observer.

Chief of Staff is your default point of contact. Specialist leads normally report material outcomes, blockers, unfinished work and decisions to CoS; bounded workers report to their assigning lead. The assigning lead reviews delegated workers’ handovers against the assignment, evidence and applicable standard before accepting or integrating their work. You can commission a specialist directly. CoS coordinates the wider picture without becoming a routine approval gate.

A role defines responsibility; an agent performs assigned work; a skill supplies a reusable method. Research, network intelligence, ATS collection, review and media production can serve several roles. Other leads can use production skills directly within their assignments; Creative Director is not a compulsory approval gate for every asset. Perception Check is a CoS-coordinated workflow, not another role.

Keep one maintained definition of each shared method and link to it. Each project retains its own brief, evidence, permissions, assets and current state. Role names and shared skills do not grant access or widen delegated authority.

Each contract names useful methods and resources by trigger and states when they are required or conditional. These routes are not an exclusive capability list. [Sense of Taste](skills/sense-of-taste/SKILL.md) supports judgement within the pair; [Community Research](skills/community-research/SKILL.md) serves harness-engineering questions across roles. [LISTS.md](LISTS.md) indexes maintained resources. Specialist acceptance, CoS integration and Engineering's whole-system scrutiny keep their separate purposes.

For delegated writing, editing, review or advisory work, the assignment names [Writing and Editing](skills/writing-and-editing/SKILL.md) and its [Writing and Editorial Standards](skills/writing-and-editing/references/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md), and supplies the relevant brief, source locations and accepted corrections. The worker reads the method and standard rather than relying on the parent’s context. Keep detailed editorial rules in the shared package.

## What a role contract defines

Each contract specifies the results the role is accountable for delivering within its remit, its contribution to the relevant governing outcome, and how that contribution is assessed through shared units of account. Maintained briefs hold current baselines, targets, timeframes, quality conditions and results.

The contract also names responsibilities, adjacent-role boundaries, relevant methods and knowledge, authority, record owners, reporting and handoffs, completion conditions, continuity and review. Link shared rules and methods rather than copying them into every contract. Each assignment supplies its concrete scope, permitted actions, effort and checkpoint.

Use the [seven-section template](roles/ROLE-TEMPLATE.md). It specialises the common contract for the role and connects its work to maintained evidence and feedback. Current account bindings, numerical targets and working records stay in the deployment's private briefs.

Each role reviews its work and retains useful learning. Head of Harness Engineering adds independent scrutiny of the wider system; existing task and change owners retain their decisions.

Before treating a role as operational, exercise both participants: retrieve and apply the accepted guidance and original sources, exchange consequential findings during work, reconcile new human steering, complete useful authorised work and restore both perspectives after interruption. Verify that uncertain effects are checked before retrying and that one coherent handover reaches its owner. Use the [runtime acceptance conditions](docs/operating-contract.md#runtime-acceptance). An agreed role type, two named agents or a saved document alone does not establish that capability.
