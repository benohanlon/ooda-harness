# Roles

Roles carry continuing responsibility for outcomes. This directory summarises the nine agreed role types in OODA Harness. Detailed role contracts and their runtime loading routes remain under development.

The [README](README.md) explains the wider operating model. [SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md) maps shared methods and their implementation status.

## First implementation

Start with Chief of Staff and Head of Talent completing one useful assignment. Head of Harness Engineering owns the build and its reliability. Add further roles when continuing work needs a distinct owner. The nine role types below do not imply nine deployed or always-on agents.

## Agreed responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Chief of Staff** | Maintain the whole picture: orientation, priorities, coordination, challenge and review. Integrate specialist findings and bring you progress, recommendations and decisions that need your judgement. |
| **Head of Talent** | Own the career remit, including opportunities, recruiters, assessment, applications, interviews and progression. This combines the earlier Career Lead, Executive Recruiter and Executive Career Agent responsibilities. |
| **Head of Business Development** | Win worthwhile fractional and client work, from understanding buyer problems and selecting an offer through qualification, proposals, terms and commercial follow-through. |
| **Creative Director** | Own creative direction and quality across writing, graphics, memes, animation and video. Preserve argument, research fidelity, voice and meaning, while keeping each publication or project's brief, brand, evidence and assets distinct. |
| **Project Lead — per project** | Own a project's agreed outcomes, scope, workplan, dependencies, stakeholders, delivery and results. Use the same role definition with a separate private brief and state for each project. |
| **Caseworker** | Manage continuing cases involving rules, laws, standards or complex bureaucratic processes. Maintain each case's evidence, correspondence, deadlines, decisions and next action. |
| **Head of Harness Engineering** | Build and maintain reusable definitions, integrations, collectors, runtime mapping, checks and reliability. Conduct bounded independent reviews of whole-system alignment, drift and design effectiveness. |
| **Personal Assistant** | Handle general administration, appointments, practical requests and routing. Continuing evidence-led cases belong to Caseworker; wider judgement and coordination belong to Chief of Staff. |
| **Head of Finance** | Own financial planning, including portfolio and risk analysis. Its contract will define the detailed remit and authority. Keep records and risk calculations separate for personal holdings and funded challenges. |

Head of Business Development wins client mandates; Project Leads deliver the agreed work. Where career and commercial opportunities overlap, assign one current owner and preserve the existing history.

## Coordination and shared skills

Chief of Staff is your default point of contact. Specialist leads normally report material outcomes, blockers, unfinished work and decisions to CoS; bounded workers report to their assigning lead. You can commission a specialist directly. CoS coordinates the wider picture without becoming a routine approval gate.

A role defines responsibility; an agent performs assigned work; a skill supplies a reusable method. Research, network intelligence, ATS collection, review and media production can serve several roles. Other leads can use production skills directly within their assignments; Creative Director is not a compulsory approval gate for every asset. Perception Check is a CoS-coordinated workflow, not another role.

Keep one maintained definition of each shared method and link to it. Each project retains its own brief, evidence, permissions, assets and current state. Role names and shared skills do not grant access or widen delegated authority.

## What a role contract defines

Each contract specifies the results the role is accountable for delivering within its remit, its contribution to the relevant governing outcome, and how that contribution is assessed through shared units of account. Maintained briefs hold current baselines, targets, timeframes, quality conditions and results.

The contract also names responsibilities, adjacent-role boundaries, relevant methods and knowledge, authority, record owners, reporting and handoffs, completion conditions, continuity and review. Link shared rules and methods rather than copying them into every contract. Each assignment supplies its concrete scope, permitted actions, effort and checkpoint.

Each role reviews its work and retains useful learning. Head of Harness Engineering adds independent scrutiny of the wider system; existing task and change owners retain their decisions.

Before treating a role as operational, verify that an agent can find its assignment, load the accepted guidance, use the relevant sources, complete useful authorised work and resume from saved state. An agreed role type or saved document alone does not establish that capability.
