# Horizon Scanning

Horizon scanning combines opportunity discovery and relationship management to increase the surface area for luck. We watch chosen targets closely enough to catch a change, look widely enough to find opportunities we did not anticipate, and build relationships through which people bring opportunities to each other. Each of the three loops can observe, orient, decide, act and learn.

| Loop | Starting point | Useful return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Closed — Monitoring & Follow-through** | Start with a known target, registered source, active pursuit or unfinished assessment. Compare the latest evidence with the previous state and decision. | A verified change, a next action with an owner, or a deliberate wait with a return date or event. Record outstanding coverage when the work is incomplete. |
| **Open — Discovery & Ingestion** | Start with a thesis, question or external signal. Explore unfamiliar opportunities, verify the original evidence and check for prior sightings or decisions. | A qualified opportunity, an emerging need, a useful source, or evidence that changes the thesis. Return it to the appropriate existing record. |
| **Network — Relationship Management (Intelligence & Engagement)** | Relationships compound when people remember what mattered, contribute something useful and follow through. Start from the last meaningful exchange. Shared work or a thoughtful introduction can give someone a reason to reconnect or bring another person into the conversation. | Record what each person gave, learned or promised and what came back. Use that history to recognise a timely next conversation. Look for credible introductions and opportunities people bring to one another. |

One message may serve several loops. A trusted contact might introduce a new opportunity and ask about an existing pursuit. Keep the original exchange once, link it where relevant, and agree one owner per next action.

### Apply this in a private harness

Under each loop, list the resources people actually use. For each, give its scope, accountable role, dated evidence of its latest use and a link to the live record. Distinguish an audience from a send history and an observed reply. Link to schedules for timing and to the cross-loop briefing for decisions.
