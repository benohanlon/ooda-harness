# Career direction template

Use this to create or revise the maintained `career.md` in a [private Sovereign Profile](sovereign-profile-template.md). It gives an assigned career agent a common starting point for assessing opportunities and choosing evidence. Link the completed file from the private profile index and the private role application or current brief. During a career assignment, follow that route to the completed file and retrieve the approved résumé, full cases or live opportunity records when the question needs them.

Start from the sovereign’s actual account, approved materials and original evidence. Reuse existing records and include only sections useful to the work. Keep detailed cases, current pursuits and routine application answers with their maintained owners. Follow the [Writing and Editorial Standards](../skills/writing-and-editing/references/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) and [Career writing guidance](../skills/writing-and-editing/references/career-writing.md).

## Write the origin story

Open with the work the person did and why it mattered. Use their own language. Keep the earlier experience that explains their choices. Follow one chain of cause and effect through the work they owned, what it made possible, the consequential decision or result, and how their present work builds on it. Organise paragraphs around turns in that story.

Put proof at the turn it substantiates. A before-and-after measure, dated adoption result or recorded decision can establish scale and consequence. Preserve who acted, who decided and what the measure actually covers. Use the underlying evidence when making consequential claims. Link sources for later verification and keep the story readable on its own. [The Anatomy of Story](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781429923705/theanatomyofstory/) · [The Moth’s storytelling guidance](https://www.themoth.org/tell-your-story/storytelling-tips-tricks)

## Write the career thesis

The career thesis says what the next move should add. In `career.md`, give each active direction a short summary that tells the reader where to look, what outcome the sovereign would own and what signal makes the opportunity timely. Link a separate card when the full thesis is useful on its own, for example when an agent needs its search criteria or a person needs to revisit its evidence. Keep the full thesis with its maintained owner and link each card back to the parent.

A full card can use these headings.

| Section | What to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Where and why now | The kind of organisation, what changed and why it might need this work now. |
| Mandate and career move | The outcome to own, who can sponsor it, the authority and resources it needs, and what the move would add. |
| Evidence and gaps | A relevant result and any material gap in fit. |
| Discovery and next test | Search signals, a useful next check and what would change the assessment. |

## Copyable career file

```markdown
# Career direction

<Describe what this file owns and link the current approved résumé, practical answers and evidence at their maintained homes.>

## Problems this person solves and what they want to own

<State the problems, the way they work, the outcomes they own and the authority and resources their next remit needs.>

## Origin story

<Open with what they built or changed and the work they personally owned. Include earlier experience that explains it and evidence of scale or stakes.>

<Show what that work made possible, who acted or decided and what resulted. Put the strongest verified proof alongside the event it explains.>

<Connect that result to their current work. Include what exists or has actually happened today.>

## Career message house

**Main message.** <One sentence about the contribution a recipient should understand.>

| Supporting message | How it works | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <A distinct supporting claim> | <What the person does to produce the outcome> | <Verified result and link to the underlying evidence> |

## Career thesis

<State what the next move should add. Summarise each active direction and link the maintained thesis card when it has its own address.>

## Working arrangements and remuneration

<Record forms of work, location, travel, working pattern, remuneration and availability. Preserve preferences, firm requirements and agreed exceptions. Link the maintained answer when it already exists.>

## Cases and proof

<Group approved materials and the strongest cases by the questions they answer. Say what each case shows and link to the version suitable for the intended audience. Keep routine application answers in the FAQ and full examples with their maintained owners.>
```
