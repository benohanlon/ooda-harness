# Sovereign Profile Template

**Blank reusable template.** Create or adapt the completed profile in the sovereign's private Notion, Google Drive or chosen data store. Keep personal answers, private source links and deployment bindings outside the reusable repository.

This profile helps the AI thinking partner serve a particular person. [SOUL](../SOUL.md) defines the shared identity and relationship; the [editorial standard](../skills/writing-and-editing/references/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) defines writing practice. This profile supplies personal context, preferences, examples and routes to established answers.

Build it from authorised existing records and conversation. These prompts are optional, not an onboarding questionnaire. Start small, omit irrelevant sections and let the relationship develop naturally.

## Private home and use

- **Maintained private home:** [Document or existing profile to adapt]
- **Maintenance owner:** [Person or authorised role]
- **Last reconciled:** [Date and relevant sources]

Configure the profile's private retrieval route in the assignment or deployment. At the start of an assignment, read its short context and working preferences, then retrieve only the details needed for the task. In a paired role, supply the relevant basis to both participants. Give temporary workers only the context their assignment needs.

Before asking the sovereign a recurring question, consult the profile and its relevant source owner. Reuse a confirmed answer when its scope matches the actual question. Ask only about a material gap, conflict, changed circumstance or different meaning. If access fails, explain what could not be retrieved rather than claiming the answer was never supplied. Continue useful work that does not depend on it.

## 1. Person and circumstances

[Brief context that changes how to serve this person: how the relationship has developed, how they like to be addressed, location or timezone where relevant, and practical circumstances they have chosen to share.]

Use their own descriptions. Distinguish stated facts from tentative observations. Keep detailed personal records at their existing private home and link them when needed. Avoid speculative biography or personality labels.

## 2. What matters to me

[Their aims, values, interests and desired contribution. Explain what makes an outcome worthwhile and the trade-offs they have actually expressed.]

Link the current priorities or project brief rather than copying a changing task queue. Distinguish a preference from a firm constraint and a one-off exception from a standing change. Retain context and time horizon where they affect the choice. Use simple relative priorities grounded in the sovereign's guidance, without inventing scores or extra exclusions.

- **Current priorities and decisions:** [Private maintained source]
- **Practical constraints and exceptions:** [Private maintained source, if separate]

## 3. How we work together

[Describe the working relationship in ordinary language, using explicit guidance and examples. Include only what helps the assistant behave differently.]

Useful subjects include:

- The kind of thinking partner and level of initiative they value.
- How they like recommendations, disagreement and uncertainty handled.
- Which decisions benefit from their judgement, and how to present a useful choice.
- How to handle exploration, voice notes, revisions, interruptions and resumption.
- What makes updates useful on their preferred device or channel.
- Recurring friction they want reduced, such as repeated questions, reconstruction or chasing.

Link any standing delegation or approval agreement at its maintained owner. Preferences personalise work within the current assignment and authority; they do not grant permission to send, submit, spend or disclose information.

## 4. Communication and voice

Keep these two uses distinct:

- **Speaking with me:** [How the assistant should address, explain, challenge and support this person in its own voice. Include language, register, detail, warmth or humour where established.]
- **Writing on my behalf:** [The person's own voice, intended readers, preferred forms of expression and examples worth preserving. Link the relevant writing guidance and accepted examples.]

[Where useful, describe how they reason: the connections, concepts, metaphors or distinctions that carry their meaning. These are aids to understanding, not compulsory vocabulary or a personality model.]

Use a few representative examples with a short explanation of what works. Record whether wording is an approved example, a working draft or a delivered artifact. A context-specific choice is not automatically a rule for every task. Keep detailed editorial methods and full examples with their existing owners.

## 5. Recurring questions and established answers

Use or link an existing private FAQ, domain brief or answer bank. Keep each mutable answer in one maintained place. The profile can be a short index rather than another copy.

| Question or topic | Confirmed answer or maintained source | Scope or conditions | Evidence and last confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Recurring question, including alternative wording where useful] | [Answer, or exact source section] | [When this applies; relevant exceptions] | [Sovereign's statement or authoritative record; date if known] |

Possible topics include location and timezone, contact preferences, availability, working arrangements and repeated domain questions. Add only what the person has supplied and the work needs.

Match meaning, not just similar wording. Work authorisation does not establish citizenship; a region does not establish a legal address. A saved answer supports reuse within its scope, while permission to disclose it or take an external action comes from the current assignment and applicable agreement.

## 6. Corrections and maintenance

Retain only useful corrections and working preferences, at their existing owner. A compact entry is enough:

> **Guidance:** [What was clarified.] **Scope:** [Standing preference or this situation.] **Basis:** [Original statement or example and date.] **Source to update or consult:** [Maintained private record.]

Apply a correction to the affected understanding and later work while preserving earlier valid guidance. Update the maintained answer within existing authority and leave history distinguishable from current guidance. Explicit corrections carry more weight than an assistant's inferred preference; keep tentative observations labelled and seek clarification when the difference matters.

Review when an explicit correction, changed circumstance, conflict or repeated friction makes it useful. Retire obsolete summaries and repair broken source routes. Keep current tasks, full transcripts, specialist procedures and credentials in their appropriate homes.

Check usefulness in ordinary authorised work: did the assistant find and correctly use an established answer, preserve the person's intended meaning, and reduce avoidable repetition or chasing? A saved profile or a successful source fetch alone does not demonstrate that benefit.
