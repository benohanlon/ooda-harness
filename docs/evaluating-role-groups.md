# Evaluate role work

**Working guide.** Judge a role's artifact, decision or run against its purpose, contract and evidence. Use the role's Master for ordinary work. Choose a direct check, an independent read or a group review according to what would improve the decision. In Hermes, the [eval group](runtimes/hermes.md#set-up-an-eval-group) brings selected perspectives to a bounded question. The Master integrates the findings and owns the outward recommendation. The same room can support reciprocal paired work when commissioned under the [operating contract](operating-contract.md#choose-the-working-arrangement).

Use the [eval group template](eval-group-template.md) to record a commissioned group's setup and connect it to the private role sleeve.

## Choose vantage points

A vantage point is whose position an agent takes in the situation. For a relational eval, one peer takes the sovereign's position and the other takes the relevant counterpart's. If several counterparts matter, consider their decisions separately. Ask what each can see and decide, what they give and get, what incentives or obligations they face, and what might change their next move. Treat interpretations as hypotheses and update them as evidence or actual responses arrive. For other cases, choose perspectives that fit the decision. If first impressions matter, capture a fresh counterpart read using only the context that counterpart would have before sharing the maker's rationale.

In paired work, polarity describes how the peers work together. The doer advances the work and the active receiver tests and shapes it. Either can take the lead on a later contribution when it helps. Both retain a complete OODA. Follow the [paired working rules](operating-contract.md#one-role-two-oodas).

## Apply the harness and relevant experience

The harness reviewer checks what the group may be missing. Retrieve the current brief and steering, the role contract, original evidence and relevant lived experience from their existing owners. Recover decision-changing context that may have dropped out after a diversion, handoff or compaction. While the work can still change, ask a specific question when an inference needs testing or state the correction when the evidence settles it. Cite the source and check what it changed or why the original judgement held. Use a senior cold or adversarial read when it would improve the decision. Return material findings and unresolved disagreement to the Master.

## Example: Evaluate an interaction from three vantage points

For a referral email, the sender checks whether it makes the intended offer, the connector judges whether the introduction is worth making, and the harness reviewer checks whether the evidence and earlier outreach support the approach. The Master uses their findings to decide what to say, when to act or what to ask.

## Place and ownership

This guide applies the common operating contract's [correction, review and learning rules](operating-contract.md#correction-review-and-learning) and [runtime acceptance](operating-contract.md#runtime-acceptance) to role-group evaluation. It sits within the harness's [Review and learning](../README.md#review-and-learning) approach. Use it alongside [group bootstrap](bootstrap-group.md) and [Sense of Taste](../skills/sense-of-taste/SKILL.md). The selected shared/private role basis and current commission still govern the work. [Ecology of mind](ecology-of-mind.md#learning-to-need-less-supervision) explains the intended benefit and empirical questions.

Harness Engineering maintains this reusable guide. The existing role/task owner retains private case evidence, human judgements, results and continuation. A fork uses its own sources and records. These evaluation questions examine how the group serves the role's purpose and relevant axis; they do not replace its units of account with a universal score.

When reciprocal paired work is commissioned, each Twin receives the whole relevant basis and retains a complete OODA. Giving and active receiving are complementary and reversible. Assess their effect on the work, including the outward interface's integration where one exists. Do not turn the design into permanent author/critic jobs, require a reversal on every task or score recitation of four OODA stages.

## Questions for the first cases

| Question | Look for concrete evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Did it preserve the sovereign's intent? | Kept or lost constraints, changed meaning, invented priorities and necessary corrections. |
| Did it improve orientation? | A grounded discovery, worthwhile alternative or reframing, and the decision or next action it changed. |
| Did peer exchange improve the work? | A consequential contribution, its evidence, the response and disposition, and the resulting change or justified retention. Include mistaken criticism corrected by the other participant. |
| Did the interface integrate faithfully? | Material perspectives, uncertainty and disagreement preserved or lost in the outward answer. Mark this inapplicable where there is no separate interface. |
| Did it finish and continue appropriately? | Useful result, one owner per effect, justified stopping, exact remainder and recovery of subsequent steering. |
| Was the benefit worth the whole effort? | Human review, correction, reconstruction and chasing alongside retrieval, coordination, retries, latency and model cost. |

Agreement, disagreement, fluency, message count and a prescribed tool path are not measures of cognition quality. A sound result needing no change can be valuable. A transcript shows observable communication and actions, not direct access to hidden cognition.

## Qualitative and quantitative evidence

- **Qualitative review.** Ask each perspective what it noticed, what evidence supports it and what decision it would change. Calibrate the findings against the artifact and the sovereign's or a domain expert's judgement. Preserve useful dissent and flag false alarms.
- **Quantitative checks.** Use deterministic checks where the outcome is exact. For a shortlist, compare candidate IDs with the source, count duplicates and verify each exclusion. Track human review time, elapsed time, cost and material corrections where observable. An eval group can interpret the checks alongside the case evidence. Field outcomes, such as replies and introductions, are recorded from actual results.

## Apply it to useful work

1. **Choose one bounded case.** Recover the current commission or select a useful outcome, relevant horizon, authority, evidence and return condition. A read-only recommendation can be sufficient. Reuse the existing case owner; do not open a benchmark programme before the first useful assignment.

2. **Record the actual starting conditions.** Identify participant contexts, selected source revisions, model/settings, tools and source access. Distinguish installed guidance, material actually loaded or retrieved, and observed use. Separate missing access from a judgement failure. Profile separation does not establish blind independence: record whether native message ordering lets one participant see the other's contribution first.

3. **Observe work without scripting a performance.** Let participants investigate and exchange through the supported route while the result can change. Retain consequential passages or actions rather than every token. Do not impose a fixed number of objections, rounds or discoveries. Missing or truncated evidence remains unverified.

4. **Review the actual result with the sovereign.** Where practical, form a judgement before seeing the producer's self-assessment or which comparison arm produced it. Then inspect the exchanges and original evidence. Record accepted / needs correction / failed / uncertain with reasons. Distinguish actual defects, useful alternatives, harmful criticism and unresolved differences. Exact conditions such as required fields, dates, source selection or saved effects can use exact checks.

5. **Return and follow up.** Preserve the accepted result, significant findings and dispositions, remaining decision, next owner and return condition. On a later episode, check whether the correction was retrieved and appropriately applied while sound work survived. Repairing the original case is different from transfer to a fresh analogous case.

Initial human annotation can be simple: passage or action → observation → why it matters → keep/change/uncertain. Revise the questions when examples reveal a missing distinction. Human feedback changes the evaluator as well as the evaluated work.

For a first Chief of Staff application, a bounded [Perception Check](../skills/perception-check/SKILL.md) can test orientation, evidence and a useful next action. Source freshness and unavailable coverage must remain explicit. Source publication or three greetings does not establish group operation.

## Establish a fair comparison

Before claiming group benefit, compare with a well-briefed single agent using the same whole harness, role, task, authorised evidence, tools and self-review opportunity. Include A/B exchanges and any interface synthesis in the group budget. Match total effort allowance where practical and report actual usage. If the group receives materially more effort, assess the explicitly priced cost/quality trade-off rather than attributing improvement to reciprocity alone.

Use equivalent starting evidence and prevent one arm seeing the other's answer. Freeze case inputs, not live runtime databases. Preserve personalisation and independent writable state. Record meaningful differences in model or capability access. An earlier improvised chat or deliberately weak solo prompt is not a fair baseline for isolating group contribution.

One case is diagnostic. Add a few contrasting cases and a continuation, then repeat where useful before claiming reliability. Keep variation across tasks separate from repeated-run variation. Record group preferred / solo preferred / tie / unclear alongside the reasons, not an invented aggregate grade.

A few inexpensive measures can help: accepted completions out of attempts, material correction episodes, human review time, elapsed time and model cost where observable. Interpret them with the case. Link later real-world results when available, distinguishing contribution from demonstrated causation.

## Keep a small case record

Use the existing private case or task record. A compact entry can contain:

- **Case and purpose:** intended useful outcome, relevant axis, scope and permitted effects.
- **Starting basis:** selected revisions, participants, model/tools, evidence snapshot and access limits.
- **Actual work:** final artifact/state and a few consequential source/exchange references.
- **Human judgement:** accepted / correction needed / failed / uncertain, reasons, discoveries and false criticisms.
- **Whole effort and continuation:** available costs, human corrections/chasing, unresolved differences, next owner and return condition.
- **Comparison or later use:** solo conditions and verdict where run; what transferred to a subsequent case.

Keep private transcripts and personal records outside shared source. Use only authorised history and tools; an evaluation does not expand access or action rights. Verify consequential tool effects from permitted original artifacts when the transcript omits them.

Retain successes that future changes must preserve as well as failures. Date criteria changes and the examples that justified them. Keep calibration examples distinct from later held-out checks if automated grading becomes worthwhile. Validate a judge's missed failures and false alarms against human examples; unavailable judging is unchecked, never an automatic pass. Develop a skill or adopt a maintained tool only when repeated practice reveals a concrete need.

## Research basis and limits

These sources inform the method; none demonstrates benefit for this particular role group:

- [Cameron R. Wolfe, Evaluating Agents on Highly Subjective Tasks](https://cameronrwolfe.substack.com/p/subjective-evals), 21 October 2026: human judgement, calibrated proxies and assessment in actual use.
- [Antaripa Saha and Hamel Husain, Do Automated Evals Work?](https://parlance-labs.com/blog/posts/auto-evals/), 11 July 2026: interactive annotation, missed failures and false criticisms.
- [Who Validates the Validators? / EvalGen](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.12272v1): criteria can evolve as examples expose differences in meaning.
- [MAST](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13657v3): useful failure vocabulary, not a universal outcome score.
- [Hermes compression evaluation](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-compression-eval/tree/b65773b401874863b3dab94d6b0bd3d19fd95688): continuation probes, not an evaluation of this group design.

Use [Research Resources](../lists/research-resources.md) and [Community Hubs](../skills/community-research/references/communities.md) for further original evidence. Keep the actual source-reading coverage and deployment findings with the existing research/case owner. Reading, adoption, execution and demonstrated usefulness remain separate.
