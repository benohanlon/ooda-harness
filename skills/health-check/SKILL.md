# Health Check

Check whether the relevant harness components and working methods still serve their purpose together. Use for a requested harness health check, recurring friction, consequential drift or reconciliation after substantial exploration and change. A routine edit needs only its affected checks.

[Perception Check](../perception-check/SKILL.md), including its 360 alias, refreshes what needs attention. This method examines the machinery and guidance that produce the work. [Sense of Taste](../sense-of-taste/SKILL.md) supplies the evidence-led judgement method; apply it to the affected whole. Use [Writing and Editing](../writing-and-editing/SKILL.md) and its editorial standard for the report.

## Establish the review

Recover the current assignment, outcome, accepted decisions, relevant revisions and prior findings. Identify the affected shared definitions, private adaptations, deployed instructions and actual results. Use available records and inspect consequential gaps; a summary or installed file alone does not establish application. Bound the reading and effort to what could change the judgement.

Keep the assignment's authority. An explicitly advisory review stays advisory; an active build retains its authorised repair work. A Health Check does not appoint a role, widen access or authorise merge, deployment, schedule changes or private-data sharing. Engineering maintains this reusable method and whole-system scrutiny; the current assignment identifies participants, change owners and any specialist procedures.

## Examine the work and its connections

Follow useful paths from purpose and accountability through instructions, evidence, action, results and later reuse. Compare the maintained design with the actual reviewed source or runtime. Reuse valid earlier findings and inspect changed or unresolved parts. Look for missing handoffs, conflicting definitions, stale routes, orphans and duplicated policy or state where these affect use. Distinguish a faulty component from a reason to redesign the architecture.

Examine the guidance and the review process as well as the producer:

- **Correction scope.** For a suspect rule, recover its triggering situation and intended scope where possible. Has a local correction become a global prohibition or an exploratory idea become a requirement? What useful behaviour must remain possible? Preserve deliberate authority boundaries, factual fidelity and accepted design invariants. Missing provenance warrants uncertainty, not automatic deletion.
- **Criticism quality.** Check the original evidence, prior sound work and the consequence of the proposed repair. Was the criticism valid, withdrawn, superseded or still uncertain? Do not let a reviewer's interpretation silently become an acceptance criterion. Review whether earlier repairs helped or instead narrowed behaviour, caused regressions or moved effort elsewhere.
- **Application and cost.** Distinguish retrieval, interpretation, selection, execution and verification failures before adding another rule. A read count, passing package check or statement of compliance does not establish competent use. Include the sovereign's repeated corrections and recovery effort when evidence is available; do not invent measured savings or causation.

For detailed correction, learning and later-use rules, follow the relevant accepted basis, including the [common operating contract](../../docs/operating-contract.md#correction-review-and-learning) when it governs the assignment. Preserve a commissioned role's selected revisions.

## Converge after exploration

When inquiry has opened several strands, reconcile the material findings with the current brief, later steering and accepted work. Identify what changes the decision, what remains an option or uncertainty, and what is unsupported or superseded. Preserve consequential alternatives without forcing agreement. Explain the disposition where it matters.

Consolidate reusable conclusions at their existing owner, retaining source, scope, acceptance state and later corrections. Keep exploratory history as evidence. Merge duplicate instructions where useful, repair affected routes and carry authorised changes into the actual artifact. Check that the resulting parts agree and remain usable together. A synthesis report alone does not complete an authorised build.

The [Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/) is a useful optional lens: explore and define the problem, develop alternatives and converge on a useful delivery. Return to inquiry when evidence changes the framing. It adds no compulsory stages, documents or new approval gate to recursive OODA.

## Repair, report and learn

Make the smallest warranted repair within the assignment and existing change procedure; use the [PR review guidance](../../docs/pull-request-review.md) for PR work. Recheck the changed result and its material connections, including successful behaviour that must survive. Otherwise return the concrete recommendation, evidence, owner and next move. Distinguish confirmed defects, plausible risks and choices of taste. Retain sound work when a criticism fails.

Lead with the health judgement and consequential findings. Give actual coverage, reviewed revisions, applied repairs, remaining uncertainty and exact continuation in the existing work record. A useful review can find no change needed. Stop when the bounded question is resolved or the effort/access limit is reached; preserve any material remainder rather than starting another whole-history audit.

Check consequential adjustments on the next relevant authorised work, or a bounded practical observation when warranted. Saving guidance, installation, observed application and useful outcomes remain separate claims. This method creates no recurring schedule, duplicate task queue or requirement for a swarm, fixed scorecard or additional enduring role.
