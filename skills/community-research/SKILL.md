# Community Research

Answer a bounded harness-engineering question using relevant practitioner discussions, primary resources and contrary evidence. Use for requests such as “What does the community say?” about agent roles, evaluations, skills, context, memory or runtime methods. Any assigned role may use it. Research Lead maintains the method.

## Inputs and scope

Establish the question or exploratory field, intended recipient and use, relevant dates, available sources, effort limit and stopping condition. A role assignment supplies its governing contract, accepted steering and destinations. This skill does not activate a role, create its pair or grant additional authority.

Read the relevant entries in [Community Hubs](references/communities.md). This reference travels with the package as a bundled copy of the harness's `lists/community-hubs.md`. In a full harness, [Lists](../../LISTS.md) is the complete resource catalogue. Keep the bundled copy aligned when the canonical collection changes. For another domain, identify an appropriate source map and report the coverage gap; the current directory does not establish coverage of every community.

## Method

**Start from the decision.** Recover the current brief and prior relevant findings. Identify what evidence could change the recommendation. For exploration, preserve useful discoveries within the agreed bounds without turning every interesting link into another assignment.

**Follow substantive trails.** Search relevant community routes and authorised private material when supplied. Follow a useful post through its author continuations, substantive replies, original paper, code, documentation or implementation. Inspect failures, constraints and competing approaches as well as success claims. Record the actual corpus and access limits. A ranked reply view, search result or bookmark sample is not a complete conversation or community census.

**Read the evidence that bears on the claim.** Distinguish author statements, participant reports, our interpretations and observed tests. Inspect consequential original passages or implementation details. Record version, publication date and reading coverage. A saved date is not a publication date. If the original is unavailable, identify the secondary basis and its limit.

**Test the synthesis while work proceeds.** In a paired role, exchange observations and counterevidence through its operating contract. Both participants can revise the question or interpretation. Check whether apparently independent reports share one source. Popularity, agreement and repeated launch claims do not establish performance. Preserve a useful disagreement rather than forcing a common verdict.

**Return an application.** Explain what the findings change for this question, what remains uncertain and who can act on them. Suitable outcomes include a decision, bounded trial with acceptance criteria, retained reference, better question or explicit no-action recommendation. Separate documented capability from installation, runtime behaviour and demonstrated value in this harness.

## Delivery and retention

Lead with the answer and implication. Include the important original links, contrary evidence, reading/access limits and proposed next owner. Use a compact evidence table when it helps comparison. For a retained finding, preserve:

- The original source and relevant passage or code location, plus the discussion that led to it.
- The question it serves, publication/version information and what was actually read or tested.
- The author's claim, our interpretation, material limitations and recommendation.
- The recipient, decision or proposed application, expected feedback and return condition.

Save selected knowledge only to the destination authorised by the assignment. Keep private bookmarks and account history in their existing records. Propose changes to the maintained community reference when a route becomes useful or obsolete. Listing a source does not adopt its method.

Stop when the question is answered to the agreed standard or the effort/access limit is reached. Return the exact unresolved question and next useful evidence. Research does not authorise outreach, joining communities, subscriptions, installation or new schedules.

## Harness dependencies

For authored reports, the harness uses [Writing and Editing](../writing-and-editing/SKILL.md) and its editorial standard. Meaningful paired review uses [Sense of Taste](../sense-of-taste/SKILL.md). These sibling packages must be supplied when required by the assignment; they are not embedded here. If unavailable, state the missing dependency and any resulting acceptance limit. A standalone research task can use this method without creating a role pair.
