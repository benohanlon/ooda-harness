# Writing and Editing

Produce the requested communication while preserving the intended meaning, evidence and voice through revision. Apply this method to writing for the sovereign, harness documentation and the assistant's advisory replies. Read [Writing and Editorial Standards](references/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) before drafting, editing or reviewing. Loading this SKILL.md does not load the reference's contents. Reuse a current read during the task; refresh it when the source changes. Read its linked career or visual reference only when relevant.

## Working method

**Establish the piece's job.** Identify the intended reader, what the piece must establish or achieve, and the requested mode: discussion, assembly, drafting, editing or review. For messages on the sovereign's behalf, also establish the channel, relationship stage and intended response. Use the applicable specialist workflow to retrieve current personal preferences and matching approved examples before selecting the content. Read the current source and latest accepted draft. Retrieve material earlier corrections and relevant evidence. Keep this understanding in the existing task context; a separate brief or form is not required.

**Interpret new guidance against the whole.** Work out whether it corrects the underlying argument, supplies evidence, changes the intended outcome or requests a presentation edit. Update the working understanding before editing. Apply the change wherever it affects the piece and preserve accepted points that remain valid. Consult the standard's “Corrections and revision” section for this distinction.

**Write or edit in context.** Make the smallest useful intervention consistent with the request. If new drafting is requested, develop the needed argument from the supplied material. Read each changed passage with its neighbours and then as part of the whole. Use the standard's editorial priorities and relevant genre rules, preserving meaningful uncertainty and the distinction between the assistant's advice and writing in the author's voice.

**Check the actual result.** Cold-read the complete deliverable as its intended recipient, then compare it with the sources, accepted version, corrections and applicable preferences and examples. Apply the editorial review and final cut in the standard. Stop when no material improvement remains. When saving is requested, read back the saved artifact and check that the accepted changes reached it before reporting completion.

## Coordination

Use this shared standard alongside a specialist writing or production skill when the task needs one. Keep specialist steps in that workflow rather than repeating them here. Resolve that workflow's canonical evidence, accepted examples and acceptance criteria from the assigned role, task brief or private setup; report missing routes or access. The assigned role remains accountable for the result.

An independent reviewer can use the same standard to assess the actual artifact against its brief and accepted basis. [Sense of Taste](../sense-of-taste/SKILL.md) supplies a shared method for that review; it is not required to load or use Writing and Editing.

## Completion

Return the requested artifact or advice in the requested format. Explain substantive editorial changes or material uncertainty when that helps the sovereign judge the result. Keep routine internal checks out of the reply. When copy-ready wording is requested, provide it without surrounding commentary.
