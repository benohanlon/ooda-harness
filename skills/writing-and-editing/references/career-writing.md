# Career writing

Use this reference for outreach, applications, cover letters and interview narratives. Apply it with the shared [Writing and Editorial Standards](EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md).

## Purpose and evidence

Distinguish a recruiter introduction from qualification of a specific role. A recruiter introduction states the target remit, problems solved and relevant proof, then asks whether the recipient has a suitable mandate. Do not substitute a request for market calibration or advice when the intent is to find work.

Resolve the canonical source locations from the assigned role, current task brief or private setup. Retrieve the current career brief, verified evidence, constraints, exact role and relevant same-employer history, together with approved examples and proof-selection guidance. Use the applicable career workflow for evaluation, application and sending. This package carries the editorial method; private source locations and evidence remain with that workflow. If a required source is inaccessible or its location is unspecified, name the gap, distinguish what was reviewed from what was unavailable, and use only supported claims. Do not treat this generic reference as a substitute for the evidence or reviewed examples.

Before drafting, state the employer’s actual needs plainly, usually in 2–3 categories. Retrieve and use relevant approved examples and the current problem-to-proof map, including its contextual weighting. Report a missing or inaccessible source rather than silently skipping it. Continue useful work that the available evidence supports.

Check the draft against Ben’s builder contribution: growth, adoption and distribution through teams and developer ecosystems; hands-on first, then building the team around what works; capabilities beyond headcount through community, DevRel, partnerships and AI. Where the operating model matters, preserve how numbers before strategy and system-wide units of account create alignment within teams and coordination at scale across ecosystems. Select what this recipient needs rather than inserting the full statement every time. Developer ecosystems describe a core contribution, not an exclusive eligibility gate.

## Select and explain proof

Compare relevant proof options for personal action, realised outcome, scale and transfer to the proposed work. Include programme evidence when relevant, rather than relying only on résumé metrics. Choose the lead proof for its relevance and strength, with supporting proof when useful.

Select a proof story: starting scale or stakes → Ben’s intervention → valuable outcome → significance. Preserve relevant numbers that establish scope and explain how quantity becomes quality, influence or delivery. Make each selected proof point intelligible: identify the organisation, product or programme, Ben’s action, result, period and relevant denominator where needed. Do not force every detail into every sentence.

Establish the level of the mandate before selecting evidence. Lead with a relevant system-wide outcome when the work requires ownership at that level, then explain the mechanisms and supporting metrics. Does the opening establish the ability to own the whole mandate, or only execute one activity within it? Neither the largest number nor the closest keyword automatically wins.

Compare funnel stages only when their definitions, dates and cohorts match. A quality filter is not automatically behavioural conversion. Participant count is not the same as network influence; a relationship is not control of participants’ assets or votes. A testnet journey count must retain its testnet scope, action definition and relevant denominator. Programme designs demonstrate an approach, not delivered results.

Keep exact source values in the working record. Use permitted narrative rounding only when the meaning remains accurate. Explain significance with a sourced baseline, comparator or share of the relevant system where available. Never use an isolated number or treat an illustrative figure as verified evidence.

Preserve belief, inference and verified evidence as distinct. Pass when each selected claim has a source and a clear connection to the problem.

## Delivery

Keep approved wording and approval or sending status distinct. When an application or message is to be delivered, use the applicable workflow to verify the actual questions and limits, attachment versions, final contents and confirmed result. A request to prepare copy does not itself request submission. Before declaring copy ready, compare the actual delivery artifact against the latest accepted wording.
