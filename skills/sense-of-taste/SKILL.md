# Sense of Taste

Judge whether the actual work serves the sovereign's intention and its place in the whole. Taste includes substance, usefulness, coherence and worthwhile possibilities. It extends beyond stylistic polish or a score.

Use this method for direct self-review, an explicitly assigned independent review, or within a commissioned doer/giver and observer/active-receiver pair. Either participant can investigate, challenge and revise a judgement. The skill supplies the review method; it does not create the pair, appoint a role or grant action or approval authority. It can also support an explicitly assigned independent review.

## Inputs and dependencies

Retrieve the current assignment, original brief, accepted steering, actual artifact or state, and relevant evidence. Include the wider purpose, what must survive, acceptance conditions, review scope and effort bound. For harness work, retrieve the applicable [README](../../README.md), [SOUL](../../SOUL.md) and assigned role contract. Check adherence to their relevant guidance. Use the [common operating contract](../../docs/operating-contract.md) for paired participation, action ownership, continuity and learning.

The assignment supplies domain standards, private source bindings and any missing access. For prose, also use [Writing and Editing](../writing-and-editing/SKILL.md) and read its editorial standard. These are explicit context dependencies: an isolated copy of this skill does not include them. Report a missing dependency and its effect on the review; do not claim an unchecked standard was met.

## Working method

**Recover the current meaning.** Distinguish a presentation request from a change to the argument, outcome or authority. Apply accepted corrections to your understanding of the whole. Preserve earlier decisions and successful work that remain valid. Neither participant's provisional interpretation silently replaces the accepted brief.

**Read the originals and the actual result.** Form an initial view before reading the producer's verdict where useful. Inspect relevant source passages, actions, artifacts and resulting state. A producer's completion report is a claim to check. State unavailable evidence and exact unchecked scope. Distinguish intentional exclusions from missing work, and silence from a known outcome.

**Challenge and preserve.** Cold-read as the intended recipient. Then test the strongest plausible objection against the brief and evidence. Look for misunderstood intent, lost relationships, unsupported conclusions, conflicting instructions, avoidable effort and effects elsewhere in the system. Check whether the relevant guidance was actually used before proposing another rule. Identify sound work and useful exclusions that must survive. Retrieve an existing precedent or investigate an alternative when it could materially change the judgement within the assignment's bounds. Do not manufacture criticism or reopen settled choices to demonstrate independence.

**Contribute while work proceeds.** Return consequential evidence, changed interpretations or a useful possibility when it can affect the next decision. The active receiver can discover something the producer has missed; execution can also correct the receiver. A final verdict alone does not demonstrate reciprocal participation. Use the assigned checkpoint and communication route, and distinguish a queued contribution from one received and applied.

**Give a finding the producer can use.** For each material finding, identify the relevant requirement, exact evidence, consequence and smallest useful repair or alternative. Separate verified defects, judgement calls and unresolved questions. Keep the review proportionate: neither an objection count nor forced agreement measures its value.

**Reconcile and recheck.** Consider counterevidence from the producer and revise or withdraw mistaken criticism. Record the material disposition: repair, retain with reasons, investigate within scope, or return a decision to its existing owner. Keep valid alternatives and unresolved dissent visible. The assigned action owner implements authorised changes; the reviewer gains no veto. Check the changed artifact and its affected dependencies against the current brief, including what was supposed to survive. Preserve specialist acceptance and whole-system scrutiny where their separate purposes apply.

## Completion and learning

Return a concise judgement with the evidence that changes a decision, sound work to retain, material repairs and remaining limits. A sound result can require no change. Stop when no material improvement remains within the agreed review scope, or when the effort bound is reached. State an unresolved acceptance failure or uncertainty instead of declaring a pass.

Retain consequential findings and their dispositions in the existing work record. Distinguish improving execution under the current brief from questioning the framing, assumptions or standard itself. The sovereign's feedback can clarify what good means; changes to governing aims follow the existing authority route. Apply accepted learning through its maintained owner and retrieval trigger, then check its use on later relevant work. A completed review, saved lesson or agreement between agents does not establish improved outcomes.
