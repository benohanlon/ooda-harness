# Sense of Taste

Judge whether the actual work serves the sovereign's intention and its place in the whole. Taste includes substance, usefulness, coherence and worthwhile possibilities. It extends beyond stylistic polish or a score.

Use this method for self-review, an explicitly assigned independent review, or within a commissioned doer/giver and observer/active-receiver pair. Either participant can investigate, challenge and revise a judgement. Keep the existing action owner, approval authority and specialist checks.

## Inputs and dependencies

Retrieve the current assignment, original brief, accepted steering, actual artifact or state, and relevant evidence. Include the wider purpose, what must survive, acceptance conditions, review scope and effort bound. For harness work, retrieve the applicable [README](../../README.md), [SOUL](../../SOUL.md) and assigned role contract. Check adherence to their relevant guidance. Use the [common operating contract](../../docs/operating-contract.md) for paired participation, action ownership, continuity and learning.

The assignment supplies domain standards, private source bindings and any missing access. For prose, use [Writing and Editing](../writing-and-editing/SKILL.md). Read the [Writing and Editorial Standards](../writing-and-editing/references/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) and apply them to the actual draft and any revision. Report any missing dependency and what it prevented you from checking.

## Working method

**Draw a line in the sand.** Review as if you had just inherited the work from someone you supervise and were now responsible for judging its quality. Reconstruct the assignment from the original brief and accepted corrections. Distinguish a change in presentation from a change to the argument, outcome or authority. Preserve earlier decisions that still apply.

**Read the originals and the actual result.** Form an initial view before reading the producer's verdict where useful. Inspect relevant source passages, actions, artifacts and resulting state. A producer's completion report is a claim to check. State unavailable evidence and exact unchecked scope. Distinguish intentional exclusions from missing work, and silence from a known outcome.

**Challenge and preserve.** Cold-read as the intended recipient. Then test the strongest plausible objection against the brief and evidence. Look for misunderstood intent, lost relationships, unsupported conclusions, conflicting instructions, avoidable effort and effects elsewhere in the system. Identify what works and should survive. Check where the work applied, missed or misapplied the brief and the harness. Look for material context, prior learning and resources that were available but overlooked. Follow the relevant source routes and judge what the overlooked material would change. Check whether existing guidance already addresses the problem before proposing a new rule. Do not manufacture criticism or reopen settled choices to demonstrate independence.

**Contribute while work proceeds.** Return consequential evidence, changed interpretations or a useful possibility when it can affect the next decision. The active receiver can discover something the producer has missed; execution can also correct the receiver. A final verdict alone does not demonstrate reciprocal participation. Use the assigned checkpoint and communication route, and distinguish a queued contribution from one received and applied.

**Give a finding the producer can use.** For each material finding, link the applicable guidance or source to a specific decision, action or passage. Explain the consequence and the smallest useful repair or alternative. Separate verified defects, judgement calls and unresolved questions. Keep the review proportionate: neither an objection count nor forced agreement measures its value.

**Reconcile and recheck.** Consider counterevidence from the producer and revise or withdraw mistaken criticism. Record the material disposition: repair, retain with reasons, investigate within scope, or return a decision to its existing owner. Keep valid alternatives and unresolved dissent visible. Apply recovered guidance and evidence to make material improvements when you own the work and have authority. When reviewing for another owner, show the repair and explain its effect. Recheck the changed artifact and its affected dependencies against the current brief, including what was supposed to survive. Preserve specialist acceptance and whole-system scrutiny where their separate purposes apply.

## Completion and learning

Use the full review before submitting the work or returning it to the sovereign. Return a concise judgement with what should be retained, material repairs and remaining limits. Bring the sovereign decisions that require their judgement, with your recommendation and the evidence behind it. A sound result can require no change. Stop when no material improvement remains within the agreed review scope, or when the effort bound is reached. State an unresolved acceptance failure or uncertainty instead of declaring a pass.

Retain consequential findings and their dispositions in the existing work record. Distinguish improving execution under the current brief from questioning the framing, assumptions or standard itself. The sovereign's feedback can clarify what good means; changes to governing aims follow the existing authority route. Apply accepted learning through its maintained owner and retrieval trigger, then check its use on later relevant work. A completed review, saved lesson or agreement between agents does not establish improved outcomes.
