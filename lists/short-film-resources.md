## Story, performance and film language

- [Pixar in a Box: storytelling](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling) and [film grammar introduction](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/hass-storytelling/storytelling-pixar-in-a-box/ah-piab-film-grammar/v/film-grammar-overview) — Character, story structure, staging, framing and the combined use of picture, editing and sound. Retrieve when a sequence illustrates messages but lacks a character's action and response.

## Continuity and sound

- [StudioBinder: continuity editing](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/what-is-continuity-editing-in-film/) — Eyelines, screen orientation and matching action across cuts. Retrieve before directing coverage of one event from several angles.
- [Adobe: J-cuts and L-cuts](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/trim-clips/perform-j-cuts-and-l-cuts.html) — Let audio anticipate a picture cut or continue across it. Retrieve when individually sound clips still feel disconnected.

## Editorial and generated motion

- [Blender Studio: Project Gold storyboarding](https://studio.blender.org/blog/project-gold-storyboarding-in-blender/) and [editorial overview](https://studio.blender.org/tools/pipeline-overview/pre-production/editorial) — Thumbnails, animatic and progressive replacement in a master edit. Retrieve before polishing isolated shots.
- [Runway: image-to-video prompting](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/48324313115155-Image-to-Video-Prompting-Guide) — Describe subject, environment and camera movement, with understandable timing. Includes a last-frame continuation route. Retrieve before generating a shot or continuing an action.
- [Runway: image references](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/40042718905875-Creating-with-Gen-4-Image-References) — Build starting frames from exact subject and scene references. Retrieve before changing viewpoint, pose or setting.

## Reusable assets and production records

- [3Blue1Brown: production instructions](https://github.com/3b1b/videos/blob/306a1346a356c7a7b097875ceb51c5cd67d0a56c/CLAUDE.md) — Reuse cast/components and preview scenes. Retrieve when a new film should build on an existing visual library.
- [Official Comfy workflow templates](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/blob/main/README.md) — Pair a recipe with dependencies and a preview. Retrieve when saving a generation method for later use.
- [Baoyu: comic workflow](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-skills/blob/main/skills/baoyu-comic/SKILL.md) and [partial revisions](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-skills/blob/main/skills/baoyu-comic/references/partial-workflows.md) — Distinguish identity from style references and revise selected pages or shots. Retrieve when a local correction risks changing other artwork.
- [Penpot: contribution guidance](https://github.com/penpot/penpot-files/blob/be9b1c33ff7546f2a5246b4c52a96f1183c2fe35/CONTRIBUTING.md) — Editable source, provenance and reviewed library selection. Retrieve when promoting a candidate into shared assets.

## Source notes and coverage

**Maintained owner:** Creative Director, within the existing role responsibility for creative methods. This names the definition's owner, not a deployed role. Project briefs, permissions, selected media, current facts and results stay with their existing private owners.

**Coverage date:** 23 October 2026. Read the original source when the relevant production question arises. Sources have different jobs: a production-record convention does not establish skill in performance or editing.

| Source | Reading scope and access | Adaptation and limits |

The film-craft additions address action, performance, continuity and sound. The original repository examples chiefly support organisation, reuse and review. Both are useful, but their usefulness must be checked in the resulting film.

Keep one maintained shelf here. The workflow links to it; private projects retain their worked examples and dated evidence. Retention does not establish adoption, installation, successful reuse or viral reach.
