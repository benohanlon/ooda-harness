# Role Resources

Selected resources for accepted and proposed shared roles; each contract records its own status. The role contracts retain their chosen interpretations and operating authority. This shelf makes their reference material visible from [all lists](../LISTS.md). Role owners maintain relevance with Research Lead. These retained source notes do not claim a new complete reading of every work.

## Chief of Staff

Use the [Chief of Staff contract](../roles/chief-of-staff.md) for the role's application and retrieval context.

[a16z, How to Hire a Chief of Staff](https://a16z.com/newsletter/how-to-hire-a-chief-of-staff/) informs complementarity, strategic partnership, follow-through, coordination and protecting attention without hiding information. The fiduciary framing and paired-agent design are harness choices. The article is background, not a runtime dependency.

- [The Archetype Lens, Magician](https://archetypelens.com/archetype/magician/) includes the film's Baldwin among its examples. Use its treatment of the insightful mentor and Sage to examine understanding of people, patience and measured intervention. Read it as a literary framework and apply the character and authority given in this contract.

- [William of Tyre, Baldwin IV Becomes King of Jerusalem](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/source/tyre-cde.asp) is a historical primary source by Baldwin's tutor. Read Section 9 for his portrait of Baldwin's learning, memory and receptiveness to advice. Keep that historical testimony distinct from the film's characterisation.

- [Bernard Hamilton, The Leper King and His Heirs](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/leper-king-and-his-heirs/0F31E1FB132285680B59FE284BD5B6CE) is further historical reading from Cambridge University Press. Consult the relevant passages when investigating Baldwin's circumstances and decisions. Check the actual text before drawing conclusions from it.

## Research Lead

Use the [Research Lead contract](../roles/research-lead.md) for the role's application and retrieval context.

- **Gregory Bateson, primary archetype.** [Steps to an Ecology of Mind, 1972, in the 1987 reprint](https://ejcj.orfaleacenter.ucsb.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/1972.-Gregory-Bateson-Steps-to-an-Ecology-of-Mind.pdf). Begin with the introduction and *Experiments in Thinking About Observed Ethnological Material*. Retrieve the relevant essays on relationships, learning or feedback for the question. The [shared ecology](../docs/ecology-of-mind.md#basis) records earlier selected-essay coverage and its limits.

- **Mark Engel, 1971 preface.** In the same [edition, printed pages vi and vii](https://ejcj.orfaleacenter.ucsb.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/1972.-Gregory-Bateson-Steps-to-an-Ecology-of-Mind.pdf#page=6), examine how beliefs can shape what we notice and how their revision can open inquiry.

- **Charles Dwyer, values and perception.** Use the [recorded talk on human influence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UCBH7aaw5hs), especially the opening account of values and perception and the discussion of communication around minutes 6 to 8. Investigate what people care about, the reasons they may act and how they interpret an approach. Use this as a practical lens to examine against the actual situation.

- **Dave Snowden, strong complementary reference.** Use the [official Cynefin overview](https://thecynefin.co/about-us/about-cynefin-framework/) for decision-making in context and [7 principles of intervention in complex systems](https://thecynefin.co/7-principles-of-intervention-in-complex-systems/) when considering safe-to-fail inquiry. Match the method and permitted effects to the situation.

## Use and maintenance

Keep licensed texts, private source records and application notes in the sovereign's collections. An author page or catalogue record is not full-text access. Preserve edition, reading coverage and interpretation distinctions from the source owner. Repeated selected citations in a role are useful orientation. Update this shelf when those references change.
