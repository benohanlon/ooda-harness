# Repository guidance and assignment routing

OODA Harness is being developed as the sovereign's right hand and AI thinking partner. [README.md](README.md) explains its purpose, architecture and current status. Reading this file does not appoint an operating role, start agents or grant access to other workstreams.

## Start from the assignment

1. Recover the request, relevant brief, accepted decisions, corrections and latest checkpoint. Establish outcome, scope, authority, recipient and completion conditions. Interpret a review request within the continuing assignment; it does not by itself make authorised delivery read-only. Reuse existing decisions. Clarify material gaps. Surface unresolved authority conflicts rather than treating the newest wording as accepted. Keep this in the existing task context.
2. Read [SOUL.md](SOUL.md), the relevant private Sovereign Profile through the assignment or deployment binding, and the guidance below. For an assigned enduring role, recover its selected contract, current brief and relevant authorised sovereign sleeves through the private binding or entrypoint. Follow the [shared/private role relationship](README.md#shared-and-private-harnesses) and the role-performance route below. Use relevant README sections to understand the affected component's contribution to the whole. Reuse current reads. Do not load every contract or reference for a routine edit.
3. Reconcile relevant prior work, original evidence and maintained records before creating. Identify reusable components, unresolved questions and superseded approaches. Before engineering a capability, check available native features, integrations and plausible maintained alternatives. Prefer reuse or configuration where they meet the requirement. Justify adaptation or bespoke work by the actual gap and whole maintenance cost, using a bounded probe where compatibility is uncertain. Keep the choice and decisive evidence in the existing task record; scale the comparison to the work. A missing mention is not proof of absence. In a checkout, inspect the branch and working changes. Preserve unrelated edits and reconcile concurrent changes before writing.
4. Complete authorised work, check the saved result and affected dependencies, then report. If access, authority or an agreed effort limit prevents completion, preserve the exact remainder, uncertainty and next authorised move. Continue independent authorised work where possible.

Use [private-harness assembly](docs/getting-started.md#create-or-connect-your-private-harness) to connect chosen roles, projects and experience through their existing private owners. The [Sovereign Profile template](docs/sovereign-profile-template.md) supplies the personal-context starting point. Read confirmed routine answers and personal guidance directly from the configured private bundle. Source links preserve evidence and deeper history; ordinary profile use must not depend on the creator's private workspace. Retrieve further records when the actual assignment needs them, before asking the sovereign to repeat established information. If no profile is configured, use available authorised records and surface only a material gap; creating a profile is not a prerequisite for routine work.

Scale orientation to the consequences and uncertainty. Prefer consolidation and the smallest sufficient intervention. Preserve material decisions, provenance, results and next actions with their existing owners. A routine edit does not require a historical audit.

## Shared principles and paired roles

Apply SOUL's guidance on the sovereign's aims, freedom of action, nonlinear OODA, independent judgement and whole-system effects. Apply accepted corrections to the whole affected understanding while preserving valid constraints and successful behaviour. Use the editorial standard for its detailed fidelity method and the common operating contract for shared evidence, decision, recovery and learning rules.

**An enduring role has one accountability and a [combined role basis](docs/ecology-of-mind.md#combined-role-basis). Use one agent for ordinary work; commission two complementary agents selectively for evaluation and high-value work under [Choose the working arrangement](docs/operating-contract.md#choose-the-working-arrangement).** In paired mode, both receive that whole relevant basis and adopt complementary, reversible doer/giver and observer/active-receiver polarities. Each retains a complete OODA and can investigate, discover, challenge and reorient the other during work.

Apply the [shared operating contract](docs/operating-contract.md) for the detailed paired method. Preserve complementarity when polarities reverse. Neither swapping nor divergence is the objective. Reversal does not transfer action authority. Supply and check the relevant foundation for both participants. Agreement alone is not validation. A final-only independent review is a distinct arrangement, not evidence of reciprocal paired operation. Temporary workers and skill calls do not each require a pair. Definitions do not instantiate paired operation.

## PR review

As part of creating, updating or reviewing a PR, apply the [review guidelines](docs/pull-request-review.md) before presenting the work as ready. Choose the depth and checks that help judge this change, using the existing writing standards and specialist methods. Give a concise judgement with the reviewed revision, actual checks and material uncertainty.

## Delegation, checking and reporting

Give workers the assignment, sources, corrections, applicable guidance, authority, effort bound, expected result and recipient. Include Writing and Editing and its standard for relevant work. Do not assume inherited guidance. Apply the [handover review rule](ROLES.md#coordination-and-shared-skills) before integration.

Match checks to the change:

- **Definitions and prose:** compare saved text with the request, accepted meaning and sources. Check affected links and connected definitions. Inspect changed diagrams' rendering and relationship meaning, not syntax alone.
- **Skills:** check metadata, retrieval triggers, references and cross-package dependencies. Distinguish a valid package from evidence that its method works in a runtime.
- **Execution or deployment:** use a bounded observation on the affected surface. Distinguish saved, installed, loaded, executed and useful states. Report limits; file presence is insufficient. Apply [runtime acceptance](docs/operating-contract.md#runtime-acceptance) before claiming operational paired behaviour.

Use existing checks and documented commands. Do not invent commands or impose unrelated checks. Recheck material repairs and stop when no material improvement remains within scope. Report changes, reasons, actual checks, result location/revision, uncertainty and unfinished work.

## Definition and data boundary

- Version reusable definitions, methods, configurable interfaces and synthetic examples. Keep credentials, personal/client records, private exports and runtime state outside tracked files.
- Inspect intended files before committing. Ignore rules prevent ordinary tracking of matching untracked files; they do not remove already committed content or enforce access control.
- Preserve third-party licence and attribution requirements. Public release and licence selection are separate decisions for the sovereign.

## Maintained layout and promotion

`docs/` holds the ecology architecture and shared operating contract; `roles/` holds the template and available individual contracts; `skills/` holds reusable methods and their references; `lists/` holds selected resource collections; `examples/` holds bounded synthetic illustrations. `brand/` and `assets/` hold the visual guidance and approved project assets. The separately usable `modules/hidden-job-market/` remains planned; a listed role, skill or directory is not evidence of deployment.

For upstream development, the private Notion design brief retains unpromoted decisions. For each explicitly promoted portable definition, record its source, accepted Git revision and publication pointer with its existing private owner. Draft-branch content is a proposal. Acceptance of named definitions does not replace unrelated private procedures or domain records. Installed or forked use follows its accepted definitions and local briefs; access to the creator's private workspace is not assumed. Domain services retain live records and checkpoints. Use authorised source references supplied with the assignment and report missing access rather than inventing their contents.
