# Role contract template

**Status:** accepted reusable definition. Completing this template does not activate a role or demonstrate runtime performance.

The [ecology of mind](../docs/ecology-of-mind.md) explains the atomic role, smaller contributions and wider feedback circuit. Check for an existing contract before creating one. A shared reference contract defines reusable role responsibilities. A private contract specialises that basis for one sovereign: record the selected shared revision and deliberate adaptations, and maintain one authoritative contract per private role instance. Where no shared reference contract exists, use this template and the [shared operating contract](../docs/operating-contract.md) directly. Link the common paired rules and describe role-specific responsibilities and conditions here. The assignment supplies changing scope, permissions, effort and checkpoints.

## Assemble this role

Use this path when commissioned to create, revise or initialise an enduring role. It produces a role-specific nucleus, two configured participants when commissioned, and the connections needed for useful work. Complete the seven sections below as its maintained contract. Keep changing task facts in the assignment and private/runtime settings in their existing configuration.

1. **Establish the purpose and fit.** Recover the sovereign's vision and aims, intended recipient, useful outcome and adjacent-role responsibilities. Explain what this role enables the sovereign to understand, create, express or accomplish, and how that contributes to the [shared purpose](../README.md#1-purpose). Check whether an existing role already owns the work. Complete sections 1–2 with the agreed responsibility and boundaries. **Result:** the creator can explain why this role exists, whom it serves and what it owns.
2. **Define the shared nucleus and axis.** Establish the shared purpose, contract and accountability. In section 1, select the relevant system-wide units of account and connect the role's contribution to those outcomes. Reuse consistent definitions across contributing roles. Link the owner of current evidence, priorities, baselines and targets; retain qualitative value. **Result:** a shared governing basis ready for both participants, with unknown values identified rather than invented measures or weights.
3. **Connect methods, evidence and authority.** Complete sections 3–6 with useful workflows, method triggers, source/project/prior-work routes, permitted effects and record owners. Use [private harness assembly](../docs/getting-started.md#create-or-connect-your-private-harness) to connect relevant sovereign sleeves, including the privately customised contract, relevant project briefs and curated experience at their maintained owners. Supply both participants with the same relevant shared definitions and authorised private routes, and retrieve the context needed for the work. Identify work for the pair, contributions from existing enduring roles and bounded questions for temporary workers or crawlers. Give coordination a named owner and apply the [bounded-worker handback](../docs/operating-contract.md#bounded-workers-and-prior-work-retrieval); helpers are commissioned when needed. **Result:** an arriving participant can find what the work needs, commission useful contributions within authority and return them to the right owner.
4. **Instantiate the dipole.** When runtime setup is commissioned, reuse a configured harness or complete [Getting started](../docs/getting-started.md) if needed. Use the [Codex context recipe](../docs/runtimes/codex.md#create-the-participant-contexts) or [Hermes context recipe](../docs/runtimes/hermes.md#create-the-participant-contexts). Establish two actual participating contexts with the same relevant model specification, available capabilities, accepted guidance, contract, starting context and evidence access. Verify their required access. Record their identities and configuration privately. **Result:** two supplied contexts, rather than two names in a document. Missing access remains a named gap; continue work that does not depend on it.
5. **Connect reciprocal work and continuity.** Establish the initial complementary polarities, one action owner per shared effect, and the checkpoint/replacement route. Use supported, authorised messaging between the contexts, or have the operator or existing coordinator relay labelled contributions and accepted steering between them. Give the receiver the current artifact and original evidence routes while the work can still change; return its discoveries to the doer and carry the considered response back. Either can investigate or correct the other. Both retain complete OODAs. Use the [common exchange rules and useful reversal cue](../docs/operating-contract.md#one-role-two-oodas), specialising sections 3, 5–7 where necessary.

   Configure the record home and its owner through section 6 during role setup. Before the pair begins each commissioned assignment, initialise or reuse its authoritative task/run/case record, supply the route to both participants and verify their access. Retain the accepted contract and brief revisions, relevant measures and current steering. Keep private bindings outside the reusable contract.

   At consequential exchanges and checkpoints, retain evidence routes, each participant's material contribution and provisional view, unresolved differences, the decision and brief rationale, action owner, observed result and next authorised move. Show which contributions were received and how they affected the work. Reuse native messages and authoritative domain records where sufficient. Apply the [common checkpoint and restoration method](../docs/operating-contract.md#work-checkpoints-and-restoration) and [learning rules](../docs/operating-contract.md#correction-review-and-learning), keeping proportionate evidence rather than private reasoning or continuous transcript capture. **Result:** a usable assignment record and recovery route, with both perspectives and their effects available for review.
6. **Commission the first useful assignment.** Supply its outcome, scope, current measures, evidence, permissions, effort, recipient and return conditions using the [existing assignment fields](../docs/operating-contract.md#bounded-assignment). For the harness's first role, use the [Chief of Staff/Perception Check path](../ROLES.md#first-implementation); later roles begin with their own commissioned work. **Result:** a bounded job the pair can perform and a recipient who can judge its result. Do not start participants or actions outside the commission.
7. **Inspect the result and retain continuation.** Apply the [runtime checks](#role-acceptance-and-runtime-checks) below to actual work. Record what loaded, what each participant contributed, the useful result, material limits and what remains to resume. Review relevant quality and whole effort through section 7. **Result:** an honest operational status and recoverable next move. If one participant is unavailable, use the [degraded-operation rule](../docs/operating-contract.md#work-checkpoints-and-restoration) instead of claiming a completed pair.

This sequence describes assembly dependencies; the role's OODA remains nonlinear. Retrieve existing decisions before asking the sovereign to repeat them. Resolve only gaps that materially change the purpose, authority or result. A contract-only commission can finish at a reviewed definition with runtime setup explicitly pending. Use the [synthetic walkthrough](../examples/perception-check-role.md) to see the contract filled in; its invented participants and results are not a deployment recipe or evidence of operation.

## 1. Purpose and accountability

Serve [the sovereign or client] by achieving [useful outcome]. Name this role's accountable contribution, its dependencies and who receives its work. Explain the benefit to the sovereign or client, including relevant judgement, discovery, agency or creative capability.

Name the small set of shared success measures relevant to the work: its system-wide units of account. Use consistent definitions and units across contributing roles. Connect supporting role measures to the shared outcome, distinguishing outcomes, progress indicators and activity counts. Preserve qualitative value alongside numerical measures.

Link the maintained project or campaign brief and measure owner. That brief owns current baselines, numerical targets, timeframes, quality conditions, definitions, evidence sources and results. Retrieve existing decisions; clarify gaps that materially change the work while continuing independent authorised work.

Chief of Staff normally reports to the sovereign; other role contracts normally report to Chief of Staff. A direct commission or assignment from the sovereign can name a different recipient. Bounded workers report to their assigning lead. See the [role directory](../ROLES.md).

## 3. Operating model and execution

Describe the relevant workflows or loops and the useful result from each. Roles need not all use the same loops. Explain the role's contribution to the sovereign's wider OODA, including relevant other actors' intentions, observations and timing. State when to seek evidence, bring a decision, act, wait or return the work for reconsideration.

This role is an atomic operating unit, a cognitive dipole (the Twins): two agents around this contract, which includes the role description. Specify how the opposite, complementary relational roles/polarities—doer/giver/emissary and observer/active receiver—contribute in this domain. Their positions may reverse within the enduring role and contract. Both have complete OODAs and access to the relevant original evidence. The active receiver participates while work proceeds, and either participant can correct or reorient the other. Keep their provisional views distinct from the accepted basis. The [common contract](../docs/operating-contract.md) defines the relational roles/polarities, their recording, meaningful exchange and restoration.

On assignment:

1. Read the exact task, accepted steering and latest checkpoint.
2. Retrieve applicable guidance, required evidence and relevant methods for both participants.
3. Perform authorised work and exchange material observations. Delegate bounded, disjoint tasks where useful.
4. Check the artifact and permitted state changes against acceptance conditions and domain-specific requirements.
5. Return the result, material unknowns, next authorised action and exact remainder to the assigned recipient.

The task supplies the work boundary and effort/stop conditions. Keep dated manifests and active queues outside this contract.

Load the current task and governing procedure first, then relevant references and exact passages. Distinguish required sources from optional references. Reuse current reads and follow originals for unresolved nuance. Identify an adapted source, its relevant version and the adaptation. Do not invent unread contents or load the whole library by default.

Keep detailed notes in their existing library or source owner. Distinguish the author's argument, our interpretation, an agreed method and observed results. Reading or recommending a source does not adopt it or establish current facts. State missing required evidence and material conflicts; continue independent authorised work. Required references must travel with a skill package or declare their dependency explicitly.

## 5. Authority, delegation and handoffs

State role-specific decisions, read/write destinations, external-action boundaries and commitments by the sovereign or another person. Use authority already given for the task; ownership or tool access alone does not widen it. Apply the [common decision and action rights](../docs/operating-contract.md#decision-and-action-rights).

Assign one action owner for each shared write or external effect. Both participants can propose and challenge. Give material findings a considered disposition; return unresolved acceptance failures or decisions outside delegation to the existing owner.

Apply the [common bounded-worker rules](../docs/operating-contract.md#bounded-workers-and-prior-work-retrieval). State what this role can commission, any domain-specific restrictions, the review/integration owner and handoff route. Name the owner and reconsideration condition for material expected returns. Use an existing enduring role where continuing responsibility fits; use temporary support for a bounded contribution. Do not assume shared memory, instructions, filesystem isolation or automatic delivery. A bounded worker is not automatically another enduring role.

## 6. Records, completion and continuity

Name the authoritative task/run/case record, artifact and live data owners. Keep private accounts, connector permissions and runtime identifiers in deployment configuration. Identify this contract's canonical home and maintenance owner, and retain its acceptance/source history with the existing definition or configuration owner.

| Record or binding | Owner and route | What is retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Assignment and checkpoint] | [Existing maintained owner] | Current basis, exact completed/pending items, uncertainty and next authorised move |
| [Artifacts and live effects] | [Authoritative destination] | Result, revision and verification evidence |
| [Private Sovereign Profile] | [Private maintenance owner and configured route] | Relevant personal context, working preferences and routes to established answers; use the [profile template](../docs/sovereign-profile-template.md) without copying personal data into this contract |
| [Private source/runtime bindings] | [Private deployment owner] | Access and execution route; no credentials in this contract |

Use the common checkpoint and restoration method. Preserve each participant's relevant evidence and provisional view alongside accepted decisions. Separate attempted, retrieved, assessed, prepared, delivered and verified states where relevant. Reconcile uncertain effects at the exact destination before retrying, preserve newer decisions by the sovereign or existing authorised decision owner and derive totals from saved records.

Define how the recipient receives the result and how a fresh pair or replacement resumes. Record degraded operation honestly when one participant is unavailable. A saved file or timeout is not proof of delivered work or completion.

## 7. Review and learning

Apply [Correction, review and learning](../docs/operating-contract.md#correction-review-and-learning). Supply the domain-specific parts:

- **Outcome and evidence:** quality conditions, correctness checks, shared measures, qualitative value and maintained evidence owners from sections 1 and 6.
- **Triggers and effort:** useful checkpoints, surprising evidence, recurrent friction or promising alternatives, with proportionate effort and stopping conditions.
- **Feedback and continuation:** downstream recipient or decision/application, expected response window, pending-outcome owner and condition for reconsideration. Silence remains unknown.
- **Review and decision owners:** relevant domain expertise, competent independent scrutiny where warranted, report destination, change owner and future retrieval trigger.

Return field reports that distinguish original observations, interpretations, decisions, actions and observed effects. Preserve successes, material exclusions, unresolved alternatives and uncertainty. Treat the sovereign's corrections as evidence about the whole intended result. Separate single-loop improvements to the current method from double-loop questions about its framing, assumptions, measures or aims. Existing authority governs changes to those aims.

Keep findings in existing records and accepted lessons at their maintained owner. Check whether a lesson is later retrieved, correctly applied and useful. Current targets remain in work briefs. Chief of Staff integrates cross-role effects; Harness Engineering examines mechanisms and whole-system coherence, with qualified independent scrutiny of its own consequential changes.

## Role acceptance and runtime checks

Exercise both participants using the common [Runtime acceptance](../docs/operating-contract.md#runtime-acceptance) requirements. A fresh pair must find its assignment, load accepted guidance, retrieve and apply relevant evidence, produce one useful bounded result and resume from a checkpoint.

Check meaningful reciprocal reconsideration, accepted steering from the sovereign, preserved sound work, authority, one action owner, participant replacement and exact recovery. Show that the role's OODA contribution changes an observable decision, action or handoff. Repeating the instructions or naming OODA is insufficient.

State loading/access limits and retain actual results. Publication, installation, loading, execution and useful outcomes remain separate statuses. The [synthetic Perception Check](../examples/perception-check-role.md) walks through all seven sections and reciprocal work. The [research assignment](../examples/research-assignment.md) illustrates another domain. Neither establishes runtime acceptance.
