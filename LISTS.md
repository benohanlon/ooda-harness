# Maintained collections

Lists route an assignment to useful knowledge. Skills describe how to work with it. Work records retain what was read, interpreted, decided and observed. Start with the task and governing guidance, then retrieve the relevant collection and exact material. Do not load the whole library by default.

Research Resources, Community Hubs and Short-Film Resources use title case as collection names. Each opens with grouped links and short descriptions for browsing, followed by source notes where an entry needs context or recorded reading coverage. This adapts the [Awesome list format](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome) to retain the harness's evidence and retrieval guidance.

Use this page as the entry point for all shared resource lists. New resource collections belong under `lists/` and must be linked here and from their relevant consumers. The sovereign's own catalogue joins these shared collections to private resources without exposing those private entries upstream.

Each collection is maintained at its named home. Selected entries may also appear in roles or other useful reading routes. Community Research carries a bundled copy of Community Hubs for standalone use. Synchronise that copy when changing the canonical list, without creating another independent owner. Record the original route, purpose, retrieval trigger, reading scope, access limits and review status. Collection ownership names a design responsibility, not proof that the role is deployed.

Reading queues contain books or other material to read. Ideas queues contain possibilities and hypotheses to develop. Keep those private queues distinct. Personal bookmarks, memberships, client examples, bespoke outreach and current decisions remain with their existing owners. This catalogue does not import them or create a memetics collection.

Link stable originals or authorised full-text routes. Bundle a PDF only when redistribution is appropriate and the package needs it. Do not publish temporary attachment URLs or licensed archives. Retention and reading do not establish adoption, installation or useful results.
