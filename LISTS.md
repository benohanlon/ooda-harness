# Maintained collections

Lists route an assignment to useful knowledge. Skills describe how to work with it. Work records retain what was read, interpreted, decided and observed. Start with the task and governing guidance, then retrieve the relevant collection and exact material. Do not load the whole library by default.

| Collection | Maintained owner | Retrieve when | What it provides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Community routes](skills/community-research/references/communities.md) | Research Lead | A harness-engineering question needs practitioner evidence, implementations or contrary experience | Selected Nous/Hermes, Hugging Face and practitioner routes, with access and coverage limits |
| [Research resources](lists/research-resources.md) | Research Lead | A decision concerns OODA, learning, evaluation, human involvement or agent coordination | Selected original resources, what to read and why, with recorded coverage separate from adoption |
| [Writing and Editorial Standards](skills/writing-and-editing/references/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) | Existing Writing and Editing standard owner | Authoring, editing or reviewing prose | The shared standard and routes to career and visual references; their contents remain in the skill package |

Each collection maintains its entries once. Record the original route, purpose, retrieval trigger, reading scope, access limits and review status. Collection ownership names a design responsibility, not proof that the role is deployed.

Reading queues contain books or other material to read. Ideas queues contain possibilities and hypotheses to develop. Keep those private queues distinct. Personal bookmarks, memberships, client examples, bespoke outreach and current decisions remain with their existing owners. This catalogue does not import them or create a memetics collection.

Link stable originals or authorised full-text routes. Bundle a PDF only when redistribution is appropriate and the package needs it. Do not publish temporary attachment URLs or licensed archives. Retention and reading do not establish adoption, installation or useful results.
